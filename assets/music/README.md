# Background Music Assets

Place royalty-free or CC0 background tracks here:
- `science_ambient.mp3`: Subtle, atmospheric science/space synth track.
- `history_dramatic.mp3`: Acoustic, cinematic, or historical ambient score.

### Recommended Free Sources (₹0):
- YouTube Audio Library (Completely free, monetization safe)
- FreePD.com (100% CC0 Public Domain)
- Incompetech.com (Free with attribution)

When tracks are present, the FFmpeg renderer automatically mixes them underneath the narration at a subtle background level (-20dB) with auto-ducking.
